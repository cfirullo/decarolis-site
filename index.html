About

Francesco Decarolis Full Professor, Department of Economics
Bocconi University, Milan

I am a Full Professor in the Department of Economics at Bocconi University, where I hold the ENEL Foundation Chair in Competitiveness & Transition and direct the PhD program in Economics, Finance and Accounting. My area of research is Industrial Organization, and I mostly work on topics in competition economics, antitrust, market design, public procurement, and the regulation of digital platforms.

I am a CEPR Research Fellow and an editorial board member of The Review of Economic Studies and Econometrica. Over the last fifteen years I have held advisory roles for various public bodies, including the European Commission, the Italian Presidency of the Council of Ministers, the Italian Antitrust Authority, and the Milan Court of Justice. I have also coordinated two ERC research projects: RepCor (Reputation and Corruption in Procurement, 2016–2021) and CoDiM (Competition in Digital Markets, 2021–2025).

My research is also available on Google Scholar, IDEAS, Academia.edu, Publons, CEPR, and NBER. A detailed overview of my academic background and experience is available on the about page.
